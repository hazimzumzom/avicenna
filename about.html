Why Was Avicenna Developed?

The issue of "Medication Non-Adherence" is a serious global concern. In Malaysia, many caregivers feel anxious when they have to leave their elderly parents alone at home.

💡

"Our goal is simple: to reduce the mental burden on caregivers and improve the quality of life for patients."

Avicenna is not just a plastic box; it is a smart personal assistant that monitors, reminds, and reports on every medication dose in real-time.
